Guenda Palmirotta

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Universität Paderborn, Institute of Mathematics in the team Spektralanalysis group of Tobias Weich.

Research interests

  • Harmonic analysis on non-compact symmetric spaces $G/K$
  • Spectral analysis on locally symmetric spaces and buildings
  • Numerical investigation of $n$-obstacle scattering systems and Schottky surfaces

    • News and information

  • March 10, 2026: New preprint with Christian Arends (Aarhus University) on Patterson–Sullivan distributions of finite regular graphs.
