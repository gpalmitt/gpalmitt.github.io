Guenda Palmirotta

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Universität Paderborn, Institute of Mathematics in the team Spektralanalysis group of Tobias Weich.

Research interests

  • Harmonic analysis on non-compact symmetric spaces $G/K$
  • Spectral analysis on locally symmetric spaces and graphs
  • Numerical investigation of convex obstacle scattering systems and Schottky surfaces
  • Experimental mathematics
